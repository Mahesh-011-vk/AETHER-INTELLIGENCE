Privacy Policy

Aether AI Intelligence is a specialized research terminal designed for transparent discovery of public artificial intelligence assets. We believe in minimal data footprint and zero commercial tracking.

1. Public Data Collection

Aether aggregates purely public metadata from authoritative developer and research channels. Our pipeline collectors query:

  • Public GitHub Repositories: Verified open-source startup and research repositories.
  • Hugging Face Hub APIs: Public models, datasets, spaces, and papers.
  • arXiv Interoperability API: Open-access preprints in machine learning (cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.CV, cs.CL).
  • Open Job Feeds: Public engineering career postings published within recent intervals.
  • Verified RSS Feeds: Industry news from official lab blogs and technical publications.

No private, gated, or unauthorized data is ever indexed by our pipeline.

2. Storage and Telemetry

Aether does not maintain tracking identifiers, user surveillance graphs, or behavioral analytics profiles. Specifically:

  • Zero Third-Party Trackers: No Google Analytics, no Facebook pixels, no Segment, and no third-party CDNs.
  • Local Database Engine: Primary indexing data resides in a local SQLite file operating in WAL mode.
  • No Logging of Personal Identifiers: Search queries and filter parameters are processed locally on the client or within transient server sessions.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

This service does not use advertising cookies. Local storage is strictly limited to:

  • Bookmarks: Saved paper and entity IDs stored locally in your browser session.
  • Client Preferences: Active tab selection, filter presets, and table column density.

4. Correction and De-indexing Procedures

Authors and organizations wishing to correct entity canonical mappings or request de-indexing of public listings can submit requests directly to our editorial review team. All requests are processed within two business days.

5. Contact and Operator Information

For questions regarding data governance, contact: privacy@aether-intelligence.org.
